Declaration of AI Use
In preparing this activity, the author used Claude (Anthropic) solely as a learning aid. It was used to give step-by-step guidance on using the Ketcher and MolView drawing tools, to point out connectivity errors in the author’s draft structures, and to review the finished portfolio for formula and formatting errors. 
AI was not used to produce any part of the submitted structures or the portfolio. The author drew all Lewis structures and bond-line structures herself using Ketcher and MolView, and she designed and assembled the molecule cards in Canva. Compound names and molecular formulas came from the activity sheet itself. Furthermore, the author consulted internet sources and the CHEM 13 activity manual for chemical information.
The author independently explicitly verified every structure and formula from the activity manual, which shows on the verification strip. Any AI-generated suggestion was treated only as a prompt to recheck, and was accepted only after the author confirmed it herself. The author made all corrections to her own structures and cards, and she retains full responsibility for the accuracy of the final content.
